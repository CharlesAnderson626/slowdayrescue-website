Quick troubleshooting

Dashboard does not load

Refresh Shopify Admin, then reopen Apps → SlowDay Store Rescue. If the issue continues, contact support with a screenshot.

Store Rescue says more history is needed

This is expected for stores with limited paid-order, product-sales, or shopper-funnel history. Continue normal store activity and the dashboard will automatically use more data as it becomes available.

Conversion Funnel Rescue

Funnel activity is missing

Confirm the Online Store is active and that the shopper journey is occurring on the storefront. New product views, cart additions, checkout starts, and completed checkouts are tracked as funnel events.

Percentages look unusual

With very small samples, percentages can change quickly. Store Rescue deliberately waits for more activity before diagnosing a reliable conversion leak.

Best-Seller Rescue

Stock timing is unavailable

Inventory projections require enough paid product sales across multiple orders and selling days. The dashboard will show a history-needed message until that threshold is reached.

Review product inventory button

The button opens the relevant product in Shopify Admin so you can review the product and inventory directly.

Frequently asked questions

Does Store Rescue automatically edit my store?

No. It provides analysis and recommendations. It does not automatically change products, prices, inventory, discounts, or checkout settings.

How do I report a bug?

Email the steps to reproduce the issue, expected behavior, actual behavior, your store domain, browser/device information, and a screenshot when possible.

How is shopper funnel activity grouped?

Store Rescue uses a one-way hashed visitor key derived from Shopify storefront event data to group funnel events from the same shopper journey without displaying the shopper's identity.

QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

What does SlowDay Store Rescue analyze?

It brings together paid-order history, product performance, inventory information, and storefront funnel events to surface sales, inventory, and conversion insights.

Does it change my products, prices, or checkout?

No. Store Rescue provides analysis and recommended actions. It does not automatically change products, inventory, pricing, discounts, or checkout settings.

Why does the dashboard sometimes say more history is needed?

Some estimates need multiple orders, selling days, or shoppers before a reliable conclusion can be made. Store Rescue shows a collection state instead of forcing an unreliable diagnosis.

How does Conversion Funnel Rescue work?

It measures progression from product view to cart, checkout, and completed purchase using a privacy-conscious hashed visitor key to group events from the same shopper journey.

How much does it cost?

SlowDay Store Rescue is $14.99 per month after a 14-day free trial. Billing is handled through Shopify.

Visit the Support Center or email support@slowdayrescue.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

1. Overview

SlowDay Store Rescue ("Store Rescue," "we," "us," or "our") is a Shopify app that helps merchants understand sales slowdowns, best-selling product and inventory risk, and storefront conversion-funnel progression. This Privacy Policy explains the information we process when merchants install or use the app and when visitors use this website.

2. Information we process

Depending on the features being used, Store Rescue may process the following categories of information:

  • Shop and merchant information: shop domain, store name, time zone, app installation and authentication/session information, and other store-level settings needed to operate the app.
  • Order and sales information: paid-order records and sales totals needed to calculate current performance, historical comparison periods, average order value, product performance, and other dashboard metrics.
  • Product and inventory information: product identifiers, titles, product sales activity, inventory quantities, and related information needed for Best-Seller Rescue and inventory-risk analysis.
  • Storefront funnel events: event type, event identifier, event timestamp, page path, shop domain, and a one-way hashed visitor key used to group events in the same shopper journey. Store Rescue does not persist the raw Shopify client identifier in its FunnelEvent records.
  • Technical and security information: application logs, request metadata, error information, and other technical data used for reliability, fraud prevention, debugging, and security.
  • Support information: information you choose to send when contacting support, such as your email address, store domain, screenshots, and problem description.

3. How information is used

We use information to authenticate merchants, operate the Store Rescue dashboard, calculate sales and product comparisons, provide inventory and conversion insights, generate recommended actions, maintain the service, troubleshoot issues, protect the service from abuse, comply with legal obligations, and respond to support requests.

4. Customer and shopper data

Store Rescue is designed to analyze store-level and funnel behavior without displaying individual shopper identities in the merchant dashboard. Funnel progression uses a one-way hashed visitor key to associate storefront events from the same shopper journey. Store Rescue does not store payment card numbers or customer account passwords.

5. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell merchant or shopper personal information. We may share limited information with service providers that are necessary to host, secure, monitor, or support Store Rescue, including Shopify and cloud hosting/database providers. These providers process information only as needed to provide their services or as otherwise permitted by law.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Store Rescue, maintain security and business records, comply with legal obligations, or resolve disputes. Shopify privacy and compliance requests are handled through the app's required compliance webhooks. When Shopify sends a shop-redaction request, Store Rescue deletes the shop's stored funnel-event and app-session records that are covered by that request, subject to any information we must retain by law.

7. Website data

The SlowDay Store Rescue marketing website does not currently use advertising pixels or third-party behavioral advertising. Our hosting provider may process standard web-server information such as IP address, browser/user-agent information, timestamps, and requested pages for security, performance, and operational purposes.

8. Security

We use HTTPS, Shopify authentication, access controls, database protections, and other reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security.

9. Your choices and privacy requests

Merchants can uninstall Store Rescue through Shopify Admin and can contact us with privacy questions or requests. Shopify also provides processes for customer privacy requests that are forwarded to installed apps when applicable.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Store Rescue changes. The "Last updated" date above will be revised when material changes are published.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to support@slowdayrescue.com.

Note: This policy describes the current technical design of SlowDay Store Rescue. Privacy laws vary by jurisdiction; merchants should also review Shopify's own privacy information and obtain legal advice when appropriate.
